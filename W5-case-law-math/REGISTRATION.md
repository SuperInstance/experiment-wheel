## The question

The wheel's laws so far are empirical pairs (W6a vs W6b). W5 asks
whether the two banked laws — copies-decompose and the weak-link law —
are INSTANCES of one deeper statement, the way case law consolidates
into statute.

## Candidate consolidation

Let a draft's state be the set of constraints it satisfies. Each hand
h has a hold-set H(h) ⊆ C (what it can carry, measured solo). Then:

- **Weak-link (W6b):** after a chain, the draft holds ∩ over the LAST
  hands of what they touched — more precisely, constraints not in the
  final hand's hold-set are at risk each time it revises.
- **Copies-decompose (W6a/b):** a single hand revising its own work
  loses hold-set over rounds (regression to its generative mode).

Unified statement attempt: **a draft retains only constraints in the
hold-set of every hand that REWRITES it; revision is destructive to
constraints outside the rewriter's hold-set, and hands do not know
which constraints they are destroying.**

## The test (to seal before running)

Measurement: for each yard, measure hold-set H solo (as W6b did).
Then predict, from hold-sets alone, each braid's final score — no
fitting, pure set arithmetic. Prediction metric: predicted vs observed
per-constraint survival across W6a, W6b, W7 runs. If set arithmetic
predicts ≥80% of constraint outcomes, the consolidation stands; else
the laws remain separate case law.
