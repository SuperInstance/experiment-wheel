# OPERATIONS DOCTRINE — the wheel's laws as standing fleet policy

*Derived 2026-08-27 from W1–W11 + the four-round harness audit. Short/mid/long-term impact iteration. These are policies, not verses — each names its evidence.*

## Standing policies (effective immediately)

1. **CHECKPOINT-IN-TEETH** — every long-running lane writes `.partial` state continuously (every ≤25 items or ≤5 min), resumable on rerun. Evidence: W3b bench survived four provider crashes losing ≤25 calls each; three unlaminated lanes died the same day losing 100% of work. (W12/The Tooth; wheel git history 2026-08-27.)
2. **SATISFIABILITY SELF-TEST** — no checker scores anything until every constraint has a witness (a construction that satisfies it, or an impossibility proof marks it void). Evidence: W9 — 2 of 6 "never-held" constraints were impossible by construction; a regime of experiments ran on a broken ruler. Cost: one pre-run audit.
3. **DISK IS THE ANVIL** — any claimed count/number is verified by re-running the same command the auditor uses (`ls | wc -l`, etc.), in CI where possible. Evidence: harness audit rounds 1–4 — counts drifted 277→154 while all parties were "sure" (partly because the corpus grew mid-audit; see 4).
4. **CHALK BEFORE YOU AUDIT** — when auditing a live system, pin a snapshot (hash it) first; treat the live corpus as weather. Evidence: audit round 4 — the target grew five papers during the audit.
5. **WEAKEST-FIRST DISPATCH** — multi-model chains run weakest-relevant-hand first, best hand closes; never let a weak hand touch the final surface. Evidence: W7 (ascending 5/6 beat best solo 4/6; descending 2/6), W6b, W10 (CH-A 7/7 exact).
6. **NO SELF-VERIFICATION IN REVISION** — a revising mind must not be trusted to verify its own held constraints; verification belongs to mechanical checkers. Evidence: W8 — verify-then-fix made destruction WORSE (15%→35%).
7. **MEASURE, DON'T PREDICT** — hold-set arithmetic (L4) predicts 85.7% pooled but is not bankable for crew design; measure the chain, use arithmetic only as a prior. Evidence: W10's sealed conjunctive bar fired its falsifier honestly.
