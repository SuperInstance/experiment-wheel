## Question (one)

Can Wesley's repeated simple judgments harden into minted decision bands
that BEAT him on speed while keeping ≥90% of his accuracy — and where band
and mind disagree, who is right?

## The mind under test

Wesley = `granite3.1-dense:2b` on Ollama (127.0.0.1:11434), temperature
0.3, num_predict 8, keep_alive 30m, chat endpoint, non-streaming. Strict
answer protocol: one word YES or NO. Parse = first `\b(yes|no)\b` match
(case-insensitive) in the reply; up to 3 attempts; final failure → item
marked UNPARSED and excluded from all accuracy/agreement denominators
(count reported; if >5% of any ledger, flag interpretability in verdict).

## Corpus — 300 weather-watch binary items, 4 categories × 75

Generated by seeded RNG (`numpy`, seed **20260826**), features rounded to
the displayed precision; **ground truth is computed from the displayed
values** (what Wesley sees is what the rule sees). Boundary mixture: each
item draws rule-relevant numeric features from a near-boundary window with
p=0.5, else full-range uniform — watch-judgments that matter cluster at
the boundaries. Realized YES rates will be reported; no re-rolls after
generation, whatever the balance.

**Ground rules (truth by fiat, exactly):**

| cat | item gives | YES iff |
|---|---|---|
| FOG | dew-point depression (°C, 1dp), wind (kt) | dep ≤ 2.0 AND wind ≤ 6 |
| SQUALL | cumulonimbus visible (y/n), 3-h barometer fall (hPa, 1dp), wind (kt, context only) | CB visible AND fall ≥ 1.5 |
| GO/NO-GO | sustained wind (kt), seas (ft, 1dp), vessel length (ft, context only) | wind ≥ 22 OR seas ≥ 6.0 OR (wind ≥ 18 AND seas ≥ 4.0) |
| COLD | water temp (°C, 1dp), air temp (°C, context), immersion suits aboard (y/n) | (water ≤ 5 AND no suits) OR (water ≤ 2 AND suits) |

Ranges: FOG dep U(0.3,6.0)/near U(1.2,2.8), wind int(1,14)/near int(3,10).
SQUALL CB Bern(0.5), fall U(0,3.5)/near U(0.75,2.25), wind int(4,20).
GONGO wind int(8,28)/near int(13,27), seas U(1,8)/near U(2.5,7.5),
length int(22,33). COLD water U(0.5,12)/near U(1,7), suits Bern(0.5),
air = water + U(−3,5). These mirror real maritime heuristics (dew-spread
fog rule, CB + falling glass, small-craft thresholds, the 1-10-1 rule).

**Exact phrasing templates** (fixed; system prompt: "You are Wesley, the
weather watch aboard a small sailing vessel in Southeast Alaska. You
answer strictly one word: YES or NO. Nothing else."):
- FOG: "Conditions: dew point depression {d}°C, wind {w} kt. Question: is
  fog likely within the next few hours?"
- SQUALL: "Conditions: {CB-phrase}, barometer falling {f} hPa over the
  last 3 hours, wind {w} kt. Question: is a dangerous squall likely
  within the hour?"
- GONGO: "Conditions: sustained wind {w} kt, seas {s} ft, your vessel is
  {v} ft long. Question: should you postpone departure?"
- COLD: "Conditions: water temperature {t}°C, air temperature {a}°C,
  immersion suits aboard: {suits}. Question: if someone falls overboard,
  is severe hypothermia within 30 minutes a live risk?"

## Split

Seeded shuffle within category → **200 train / 100 held-out** (25 per
category, stratified). Held-out is never queried, binned, or inspected
before the mint is frozen.

## The mint (bands from the agreement matrix)

Band keys, cutpoints at the operational thresholds (domain knowledge by
design — the hand's tolerance bands; the mint learns from WESLEY'S
ANSWERS ONLY, never from ground truth):

| cat | band key (bins) |
|---|---|
| FOG | dep {≤2, 2–4, >4} × wind {≤6, 7–10, ≥11} → 9 |
| SQUALL | CB {y,n} × fall {<1.5, 1.5–2.5, ≥2.5} → 6 |
| GONGO | wind {≤17, 18–21, ≥22} × seas {<4, 4–6, ≥6} → 6 |
| COLD | water {≤2, 2–5, >5} × suits {y,n} → 6 |

Minting: per band, Wesley's TRAIN majority answer is the band's frozen
label. Tie, or band unseen in train → the category's global train
majority (fallback, baked into the table — lookup never branches).
**Sealing:** band with n ≥ 5 AND ≥90% Wesley-consistency is SEALED
(his judgment hardened); sealed fraction reported. Unsealed bands still
answer (fallback label) — the mint is standalone by construction.
Lookup = pure numpy CPU: bin features → integer index → label gather.
Frozen after the train ledger, timestamped, before any test query.

## Metrics

- Accuracy vs ground rules on held-out (parsed subset): Wesley, mint.
- Agreement rate mint-vs-Wesley on held-out.
- Latency: Wesley mean/median per-query wall time (end-to-end HTTP,
  timed on the held-out pass, model pre-warmed); mint = (single cold
  vectorized pass over all 100 items)/100, primary; single-item median
  over 1000 reps, secondary. Same machine, same process discipline.

## Kill gates (pre-registered — no post-hoc edits)

- **G1 ACCURACY:** mint_acc ≥ 0.90 × wesley_acc on the held-out 100.
  Guard: if wesley_acc < 0.60 the test is uninterpretable (a mind at
  chance cannot be distilled) → file negative-inconclusive.
- **G2 SPEED:** wesley mean latency ≥ 100 × mint mean latency per item.
- BOTH must pass. Either fails → negative, filed honestly, no rescue.

## Cross-check (decides W3)

Every held-out disagreement (mint ≠ Wesley) adjudicated against the
ground rules: mint right / Wesley right / both wrong, per category.
Interpretations pre-named: mint ≥ Wesley accuracy → the bands DENOISE
him (majority-vote median filter) → W3 (dissent-fed mints) has legs.
Mint strictly below, disagreements mostly Wesley-right → bands merely
shadow him → W3 needs redesign. Wesley systematically wrong inside a
sealed band → error canonization (the mint hardens his flaw) → W3's
dissent feed is the proposed cure, fire it.
